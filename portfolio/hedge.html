Hedgerow

Return To Home
Hedgerow was the first piece I wrote after I started my PhD. Saxophone quartet is the perfect medium for exploring the minute details found in dense, homogenous textures. In coaxing out these intricacies, I was reminded of the hedgerows in Devon, my home county in the UK. Though incredibly tall and dense, and from a distance uniform, on closer inspection they reveal a multitude of plants, wildflowers, and creatures. I explored the saxophone through a focus on the physical properties of its sound. Its subtle and controllable intonational fluctuations can create wonderfully variable beating that becomes a constantly shifting secondary rhythm.
I worked on the piece with a wonderful quartet made up of LSU saxophone students, Preston Hernandez (soprano sax), Daniel Lynge (alto sax), Robert Fahey (tenor sax), and Matthew Gioia (baritone sax). This collaboration culminated with a performance at the 2023 North American Saxophone Alliance Biennial Conference.
