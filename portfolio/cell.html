Return To Home
I’ve written and programmed various pieces meant to be performed solely by audiences with their cellphones. For these pieces, I create web apps that audience members can seamlessly connect to in performance situations. Despite the seemingly individualistic nature of this medium, my pieces can only be performed collaboratively and in-person. This demo video shows some footage from performances, though it's hard to capture the magic of the actual experience on video. These pieces continue to be widely performed.
I’m particularly proud of Whisper, in which audience members record themselves whispering lines of the Thomas Hardy poem In a Whispering Gallery into their cellphones and send them to a server. From there, they are played back ambisonically through speakers. A childhood visit to the whispering gallery in St. Paul’s Cathedral in London inspired this piece.
