For the first part of my compositional career, I focused on acoustic chamber music, especially on texture, intonation, and acoustic beating. As I began learning about electronics, I wrote works for live electronics with MaxMSP, and started doing field recordings. While learning JavaScript, I started making interactive cellphone pieces for audience participation, drawing on my experience with acoustics, digital signal processing, and often using my field recordings. I began combining my audience participation practice with my chamber music background, writing pieces for performers and audience participation with cellphones and objects. Most recently, I wrote, directed, and produced a 40 minute "non-opera", D, Me, You, We, for actor, AI chatbot, musicians, and audience. This portfolio shows some of the different avenues of my work, including my non-opera, my cellphone pieces, and my chamber music.
