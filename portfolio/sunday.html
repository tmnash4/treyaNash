Return To Home
Sunday Routine was written for the 2025 line upon line percussion Winter Composers Festival. Matt and Cullen invited us to write pieces that included a part for ourselves, so I wrote the piece for percussion duo and unseen narrator. The narrator and percussionists guide the audience through a sunday scene that includes storm sounds, reading magazines, and playing a dice game. The narrator introduces a theatricality that adds to the whimsy and absurdity, while also creating a clear in-performance line of communication with the audience. The musicians reinforce this communication through demonstration and collaboration, helping audience members feel secure and comfortable in an unfamiliar situation. This, in turn, allows them to freely enjoy the playfulness of the piece without focussing on perfiorming ‘correctly’.
