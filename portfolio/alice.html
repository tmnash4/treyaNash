Return To Home
Alice in Wonderland is a project that I first worked on with Irina Kruchina and Samira Awad in Unreal Engine 5.0. My idea was to create an interactive performance based on the scene in which Alice grows and shrinks. In the version, the scene itself grows and shrinks, allowing Irina to explore the space as Alice. I programmed the scene and did the music and sound design and Samira designed the visuals. See clips from that project in the first video.
I used parts of the video for a collaboration with ensemble vim and Petar Miloshevski for which I wrote new music. See the full performance in the second video.
