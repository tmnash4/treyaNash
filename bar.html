Bar To Bar

Instrumentation: soprano saxophone, image/video projection, fixed media, and audience cellphones

Year: 2025 | Duration: 6-8 minutes

Written for Noa Even

Program Note: Bar To Bar takes the audience on a journey through bars 🍹🍾🍷🥃🍺 in time. The audience is guided by a time travelling entertainer.
The performer controls the flow and timing of the piece from their score, which consists of a website for tablet and a web app for cellphone.
The piece includes live saxophone with fixed media, digitally drawn backgrounds (by my frequent collaborator Nick Barrett), a short video, and cellphone interaction.
If you'd like to try the play along section at home, you can access it here: playalong.treyanash.com
