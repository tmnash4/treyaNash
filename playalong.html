Play Along

Play along with the video by scanning the QR code with your cellphone! Make sure to turn up your volume and turn off silent mode if on iPhone or visiting playalong.treyanash.com. You will be randomly assigned one of the four instruments. Reload the page to try the other ones!

This play along is part of my piece Bar to Bar for soprano saxophone and audience with cellphones. The piece was written for Noa Even. This part is an arangement of Tielman Susato's La Mourisque from Danserye 1551. During the performance, the top part is played live on soprano saxophone. The parts for the video are performed by Noa.
